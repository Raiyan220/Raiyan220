### Focus Areas

- **Systems & Storage Engineering:** Designing crash-consistent write-ahead logging (`WAL`), block-level metadata recovery, and low-level tooling in `C`, `POSIX I/O`, and `x86` / `RISC-V` assembly.
- **Full-Stack Product Engineering:** Building modular, type-safe applications with `TypeScript`, `NestJS`, `React`, `PostgreSQL` (`Prisma`), `Express`, and `MongoDB`.
- **Applied Cryptography & Usable Security:** Implementing end-to-end encryption (`RSA`, `ECDH`, `ECDSA`, `CBC-MAC`, `TOTP`) paired with accessible, transparent verification interfaces.
